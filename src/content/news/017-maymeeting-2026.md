In the beginning of June, we organized yet another edition of [Prague May Meeting](/events/maymeeting/prague-maymeeting-2026/) -- a small informal meeting focused on recent developments in multidimensional spectroscopy and open quantum systems. The meeting took place at our institute---the [Charles University](https://www.cuni.cz/) in Prague, Czechia, from June 4 to June 5, 2026.

![The meeting venue, Charles University, Prague, Czech Republic](/news/maymeeting2026-3.WebP)

The progamme was intensive, with a variety of topics covered in the talks. In total, there were 20 presentations during the two days, with over 14 foreign attendees from all parts of Europe.Hopefully, everyone enjoyed the meeting, as well as the refreshment prepared by the students, and we are looking forward to the next edition of Prague May Meeting someday in the future.
