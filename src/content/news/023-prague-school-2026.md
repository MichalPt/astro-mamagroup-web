On September 3&ndash;4, 2026, our group hosted the [Prague Summer School of Nonlinear Spectroscopy 2026](/events/school/prague-summer-school-2026/) at Charles University.

The two-day event brought together early-career researchers, PhD students, and advanced students for a series of lectures, discussion sessions, and practical laboratory tours showcasing experimental setups in spectroscopic research.
