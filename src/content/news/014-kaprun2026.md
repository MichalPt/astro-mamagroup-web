In late February, several members of our group---[Malcolm](/group/garrow), [Matteo](/group/bruschi), [Kateřina](/group/charvatova), and [Pavel](/maly)---participated in the 16th Workshop on Ultrafast Spectroscopy and Quantum Control in Kaprun. It was a great opportunity to present our latest research and learn about new experimental and theoretical approaches. 

![Kaprun 2026](/news/kaprun-2026-2.WebP)

We also enjoyed bonding with our collaborators from the groups of Tobias Brixner (Würzburg), Jürgen Hauer (Munich), and Patrick Nürnberger (Regensburg) during hiking and skiing trips. We are leaving inspired by the many great discussions and look forward to our continued work together.
